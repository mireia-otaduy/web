Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How can I request a quote?

Fill in the form on this page or write to us at info@lcc-manufacturing.com with your drawing or a description of the part. We will do our best to reply as soon as possible.

Do you make prototypes?

Yes. Prototype manufacturing is one of the stages of our working method.

Where are the parts manufactured?

Engineering and project management are done in Spain. Series production takes place in China and our buffer warehouses are in Amorebieta and Barcelona.

Do you make injection moulds?

Yes. We have an engineering team specialised in injection moulds and prototypes, and we are partnered with a toolmaker with 2,000 m² of facilities.

Which sectors do you work for?

Automotive, electrical, home appliances, road safety, capital goods, renewable energy, agriculture and healthcare.

Do you subcontract any technologies?

Yes, when necessary. We have qualified staff and extensive knowledge of suppliers of other technologies, such as plastic or aluminium injection.
