Work process

How we work: from idea to series production

The 8 steps

Steps of the work process
01

Product and process development

We take part in product and process development.

02

Prototype manufacturing

We manufacture prototypes to validate the part before moving to series production.

03

Approval

We approve the product and the process (homologation).

04

Tooling manufacturing

We manufacture the tooling required, which shortens manufacturing lead times.

05

Series production

We manufacture in series, in China.

06

Quality control

We have quality control equipment, such as measuring equipment and a hardness tester.

07

Supply chain

We manage the supply chain.

08

Buffer warehouse

Our warehouses in Amorebieta and Barcelona regulate the logistics flow of the products we supply in Spain.
